Understanding Semantic HTML

Semantic HTML is a way of writing HTML that emphasizes the meaning of the content. It uses tags that describe the purpose of the content, making it easier for browsers and assistive technologies to understand the structure of the page. Adding images, videos and audio files to your website can enhance the user experience and make your content more engaging.
